## Meaning and limits
This specific initial-tangent, one-direction intervention does not supply the
required strong support for the proposed architecture bet. It is NOT evidence
that forgetting lacks shared-parameter interference: nonlinear curvature over
16 observations, multi-direction sensitivity and ensemble effects are not
resolved. No causal exclusivity, no disproval of growing/factorized substrates.
The diagnostic is intentionally cheap and narrower than a full interference
analysis. Its failure means architecture prerequisite is UNRESOLVED, not that
the research question has been answered negatively.

Accordingly no factor allocator, boundary learner, additional anchor sweep,
fresh sequential-law evaluator or capability trial started. Do not interpret
this as a tested failure of Expandable Predictive Factors. Actual outcomes still
support narrow adaptation gains and observed retention failure only.

Architectural obligation remains: if knowledge is frozen into separate factors,
choosing the correct factor BEFORE an action must be learned from available
history, not law IDs, labels or evaluator masks. Factor growth alone does not
provide this selection, nor establish lower future-learning cost. This must be
resolved in design, not hidden as an oracle in a benchmark.
