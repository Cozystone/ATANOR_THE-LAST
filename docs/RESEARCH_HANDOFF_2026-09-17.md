## What remains unproved / absent

- New seeds are new instances of exposed hardlink mechanics, NOT unseen mechanics.
  Two goal forms, resettable operator-provided contexts and three supplied actions
  remain a narrow world. Bootstrap is clustered by8 generated seeds, not a claim
  about arbitrary environments.
- Raw sensor fields, finite program grammar, operation syntax and goals remain
  human-provided. Max32 observed nodes; greedy/order-sensitive rule induction.
- The learner is No-LLM, but this is not yet a complete ontology-neural4D mind.
  No general persistent entity identity, continuously growing ontology, autonomous
  goal invention, language, multiple native bodies, general RSI or proven AGI odds.
- R1/R3 use persistent WorldSession and native replay. R4–R9 acquisition trials are
  isolated research runners, NOT a live lifelong acquisition/runtime integration.
- FINITE_SET_AGREEMENT is only a grammar-relative diagnostic, never verified truth,
  calibrated confidence, provenance or action authority. Trusted-host assumptions
  remain; these trials are not an external independent E5 judge.
- Native agent actions exclude operator reset/fixture and measurement construction.
  CPU costs are reported and are NOT brain-like efficiency. R9 was computationally
  worse for identical behavior; it must not be promoted.
