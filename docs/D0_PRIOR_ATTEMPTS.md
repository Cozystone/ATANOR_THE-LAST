## Important limitations of the positive precedents

`dep_agent` uses a supplied `parent_of` function over eight fixed slots. Its
selection policy is hand-written: known productive action, known two-step route
through ABSENT, untried context/state/action, then random. It learns the effect
table, not its feature vocabulary or this selection algorithm. The old result is
therefore relevant to a cheap conditional baseline, but does not satisfy a shared,
body-neutral persistent-world contract by itself.

The testimony reader is a tiny fixed lexicon/grammar including an existing-folder
guard. The later source-level policy discards all remaining claims from a source
after one contradiction. Neither a whole-source epistemic law nor broad safe
testimony integration is proved by twelve deterministic filesystem seeds.

The context-separation report records completion firing zero times, maximum depth
12 binding, stale ingest-time addresses after the tree changes, a hand-written
atom vocabulary, and only one seed/world family. Prediction improvement is real
in the reported comparison; it is not evidence of task-cost transfer, lifelong
world identity, or a general discovery vocabulary.

SPR's own follow-up locates coverage/evidence starvation: only 0.43% of episodes
had an active Structure above credit 0.20 and only 1.1% of survivors reached the
24 prospective trials needed for retirement. It specifically qualifies that
blind combinatorial birth and variation/licensing are implicated more directly
than the `Structure` datatype. Do not turn its local failure into a theorem that
symbolic structure cannot work, or erase it when proposing another constructor.

## Present boundary and stop conditions

The reviewed histories do not show the exact new `WorldSession` contract already
failing for the same cause. That absence is limited to this selected check, not a
claim that every branch or stash has been cleared.

D1 may establish only that one native world can outlive goals, that forecasts
remain distinct from observations, that budgets and pending actions survive
restart, and that an external verifier can replay the authorized native history.
It must not claim persistent entity identity from path handles, autonomy from an
externally supplied agenda, intelligence from a durable database, or measured
capability from unit-test GREEN.

Before any later learner comparison, state the difference from the old
conditional-effect learner, context splitter, and structure-birth program.
Independent downstream capability/cost evidence remains required. If the only
difference is a renamed record type or a new place to store the same table, that
is migration/mechanism work, not a fresh learning result.
